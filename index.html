About CoastConnect

Project

Participatory Web Map for Coastal Tourism Activity Registration and Community Feedback in the Western Region of Sri Lanka.

Purpose

To integrate existing tourism GIS information with voluntary community contributions, in order to identify tourism resources, visitor experiences, accessibility constraints, service gaps, environmental concerns and potential tourism development opportunities for urban and regional planning.

Explore and Filter

Filter by Category allows users to control which tourism datasets are displayed on the map. Multiple categories can be selected together, making it useful for comparing the spatial distribution of different tourism activities and services.

Quick Discovery provides a faster way to explore a specific tourism activity or service. Selecting a Quick Discovery option immediately focuses the map experience on that category, helping visitors quickly find relevant places.

Both tools respond to the current map view, so tourism locations are displayed progressively as users zoom into and explore different parts of the Western Region.

Data Types

  1. Existing GIS Data - original attributes, never edited
  2. Newly Added Places - created entirely through this web map
  3. Updated Places - an existing or community place whose information was later edited through this web map
  4. Community Feedback
  5. Community-Reported Issues
  6. Derived Planning Information

Disclaimer

Community-contributed information represents voluntary observations and may not have been independently verified. It is published immediately, with no administrator approval step, and is always labelled as community information.

Participation Limitations

Like any Volunteered Geographic Information (VGI) system, this project can be affected by: participation bias (not everyone contributes equally), the digital divide (not everyone has internet/smartphone access), uneven spatial participation (some areas get more reports than others), possible duplicate submissions, incorrect or malicious community information, subjective personal ratings, and the fact that open anonymous contribution has no admin moderation step to catch problems before they appear on the map.
